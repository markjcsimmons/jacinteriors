Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions.

Can you source within a specific budget?

Yes. We’ll propose options across budget tiers and prioritize where to invest for the biggest impact.

Do you use trade-only vendors?

When appropriate, yes—alongside retail options. The goal is always the best fit for your project.

Can you work with my existing pieces?

Absolutely. We’ll build around what stays and source complementary pieces to complete the look.

Do you help with delivery and installation?

Yes, depending on scope and location, we can support delivery coordination and final placement.
