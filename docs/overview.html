Who We Are

Design leadership with taste, intention, and spark.

JAC Interiors is a full-service design studio founded in 2012 by Andrea Putman and Timothy Mersman. We collaborate closely with every client — never forcing a style — so your home reflects who you are, how you live, and what you value.

From concept through final installation, we handle every detail: space planning, sourcing, procurement, contractor coordination, and styling. Serving Los Angeles and South Florida’s Gold Coast.
