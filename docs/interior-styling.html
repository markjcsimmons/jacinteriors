Service overview

Details that make a space feel finished.

Styling brings warmth and intention to a room—balancing scale, color, texture, and negative space so everything feels “just right.”

  1. Discovery — We align on your style, what stays, and what needs to change.
  2. Curation — We select furniture, textiles, art, and decor that support your aesthetic.
  3. Placement — Final layout, layering, and styling to complete the space.

What you get

  • Color & finish direction: paint, materials, and textures that work together.
  • Furniture & decor curation: pieces that fit the room and your lifestyle.
  • Lighting guidance: ambient, task, and accent lighting for mood and function.
  • Styling plan: layout + layering for a complete, cohesive look.
Best for

Refreshing a room without construction—new energy, better flow, and a polished finish.

Includes: in-person or remote styling support depending on location and scope.

Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions.

Can you style using what I already own?

Yes. We can build around existing pieces and add what’s missing to create a cohesive final look.

Do you provide shopping links and options?

Yes—curated selections with clear guidance on what to buy and why it works.

Is this service good for rentals?

Absolutely. We can focus on non-permanent updates like furniture, textiles, lighting, and decor.

Can this be done remotely?

Yes. Remote styling works well with photos, measurements, and digital presentations.
