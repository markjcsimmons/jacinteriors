Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions.

What does full-service interior design include?

Full service covers everything from initial concept through final installation — space planning, sourcing, purchasing, construction supervision, styling, and art placement. You work with one team from start to finish.

How do you structure your fees?

We typically work on a flat-fee or percentage-of-project model depending on scope. We're transparent about fee structures and markups from the first conversation. For a detailed breakdown, see our interior design cost guide.

Do you take on projects outside Los Angeles?

Yes. While our primary market is Los Angeles — Beverly Hills, Bel Air, Malibu, Pacific Palisades, and surrounding areas — we also work throughout South Florida and take select projects nationally.

How long does an interior design project take?

It depends on scope. A single-room project typically runs 3–6 months; a full home renovation is usually 9–18 months. We give you a realistic timeline at the outset so there are no surprises.

How do I get started?

Book a call through our contact page. We'll talk through your project, timeline, and budget — then map out the right approach together.
