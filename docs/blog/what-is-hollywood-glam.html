What is Hollywood Glam?

Think old-school glamour, updated. The modern version is less “theme” and more a confident mix of contrast, shine, and sculptural silhouettes.

5 min readJAC Interiors

Hollywood Glam is the art of making a room feel elevated and cinematic — without feeling costume-y. It’s a style built on contrast: light and dark, matte and shine, soft and structured.

Signature elements

  • High contrast: black + ivory, espresso + cream, deep jewel tones paired with pale neutrals
  • Polished finishes: lacquer, glass, chrome, aged brass, and reflective stone
  • Sculptural shapes: curved sofas, statement chairs, arched mirrors, and bold lighting
  • Intentional sparkle: a single “hero” piece instead of glitter everywhere

How to keep it modern

Choose one strong material story and repeat it. For example: blackened metal + warm neutrals, or lacquer + soft textiles. Then edit. Glam reads best when there’s breathing room.

Where it works best

We love this style in entry moments, dining rooms, and lounges — anywhere you want a little drama. In bedrooms, keep the palette calm and let lighting and shape do the work.
