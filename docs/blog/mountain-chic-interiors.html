Mountain chic interiors

Cozy doesn’t have to mean rustic. This is mountain style with polish: natural textures, tailored shapes, and a calm, collected palette.

5 min readJAC Interiors

Build the palette first

Start with warm neutrals (bone, oatmeal, stone) and add one grounding tone (espresso, charcoal, deep olive). Keep it restrained so the textures can shine.

Prioritize natural textures

  • Wool, shearling, and bouclé for softness
  • Oak, walnut, and aged leather for warmth
  • Honed stone and plaster for quiet character

Mix rustic + refined

The “chic” part comes from contrast: pair a reclaimed element with a clean-lined silhouette, or a raw texture with a polished finish. Edit everything else so the room feels intentional.

Lighting is the final layer

Use warm, dimmable sources. We love a mix of sculptural pendants, low table lamps, and gentle accent lighting to create a soft evening glow.
