A guide to the best antique stores in Los Angeles

Where we like to source character pieces — and how to shop antiques with confidence (even if you’re new to vintage).

7 min readJAC Interiors

Antiques are the fastest way to add soul to a space. We use vintage pieces to create contrast against clean-lined contemporary interiors — a home should feel collected, not staged.

How to shop antiques like a designer

  • Know your scale: bring measurements and a quick floor plan screenshot.
  • Look for patina: natural wear reads authentic; overly “perfect” can feel fake.
  • Prioritize shape: silhouettes matter more than finish — finishes can be refinished.
  • Check construction: drawers, joinery, and stability tell you what you’re buying.

Our favorite LA sourcing areas

Los Angeles has incredible vintage. Instead of listing individual stores (which change frequently), we recommend starting in established sourcing corridors and marketplaces, then following the best dealers you find.

How to mix vintage with modern

Keep the palette cohesive and let the antique piece be the “character.” One vintage element per vignette is often enough: a patinated mirror, a sculptural chair, or an old stone vessel.
