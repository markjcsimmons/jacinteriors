Service overview

A home designed for real life.

Whether it’s a city apartment or a multi-room family home, we shape every decision around your lifestyle—light, flow, storage, materials, and the transitions between spaces.

  1. Discovery & Vision — We learn your style, needs, and goals through collaborative conversations.
  2. Concept & Refinement — Layouts, moodboards, and visuals bring the direction to life, refined with your feedback.
  3. Execution & Styling — Sourcing, schedules, and final styling to deliver a cohesive finished home.

What you get

  • Concept design: moodboards, floor plans, and visuals.
  • Material & furniture selections: curated options aligned to your budget.
  • Project coordination: timelines, vendors, and decisions made simple.
  • Styling & finishing touches: art, objects, and placement with intention.

Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions.

How do you approach designing a private living space?

We begin with your day-to-day routines, preferences, and priorities. The goal is a home that feels personal, functional, and timeless.

Can you work with existing furniture or décor?

Yes—if you have meaningful pieces, we integrate them and design around them for a cohesive result.

How long does a typical residential project take?

Timelines vary by scope, but many projects take 8–12 weeks for design, plus ordering/lead times for furnishings.

Do you offer remote or virtual design?

Yes. We can run the full design process remotely with digital presentations and guided support.
