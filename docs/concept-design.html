Service overview

Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions.

Is concept design the same as full-service?

Concept design focuses on direction, layouts, and visual clarity. Full-service includes deeper detailing, sourcing, procurement, and install support.

Can you include my existing finishes or furniture?

Yes. We can build the concept around what stays and refine around new pieces as needed.

Do you provide a list of what to buy?

Yes—we include clear next steps and recommended items depending on scope.

Can this be done remotely?

Absolutely. Photos, measurements, and virtual meetings make this service a great remote option.
