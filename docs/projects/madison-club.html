Desert Elegance

Designed as a desert dream escape within the prestigious Madison Club, Columbus Way was envisioned as the ultimate weekend retreat. Working closely with both the client and architect, we crafted a design/build project that fully embraces its dramatic mountain backdrop. Expanses of natural stone, layered textures, and a color palette inspired by the surrounding desert landscape bring the outdoors in, blurring the line between architecture and environment. Custom furnishings and architectural details were thoughtfully integrated to create a cohesive, award-winning showstopper that feels luxurious yet relaxed. It’s a home that celebrates desert living at its finest—elegant, expansive, and perfectly attuned to its setting.

Creating luxury spaces that elevate everyday living.
