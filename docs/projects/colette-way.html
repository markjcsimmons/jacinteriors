Mountain Sanctuary

Calabasas Mountain View Estates Transitional Remodel

Cozy Grandeur

Perched atop a hill in Mountain View Estates, Colette Way is a 10,000-square-foot family home transformed through a complete structural gut renovation. Our vision extended beyond interiors; we created an outdoor sanctuary that rivals a boutique resort. Additions such as a custom theatre room, expansive outdoor kitchen, bespoke cabinetry, and curated art collection elevate both daily life and entertaining. The design balances grandeur with intimacy, ensuring the scale of the home still feels warm and welcoming. Every detail, from custom furniture to architectural enhancements, was selected to reflect the family’s lifestyle while enhancing the home’s breathtaking views and sense of retreat.

Creating luxury spaces that elevate everyday living.
