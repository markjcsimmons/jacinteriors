Service overview

Luxury kitchen remodeling—planned for real life.

We design and remodel kitchens in Los Angeles that feel effortless to use and elevated to look at. Our work starts with flow and function, then moves into cabinetry, finishes, and lighting that create a cohesive, timeless result.

  • Layout + flow planning: islands, clearances, work zones, and sightlines.
  • Cabinetry direction: elevations, hardware, appliance integration, storage strategy.
  • Finishes + fixtures: countertops, backsplash, flooring, paint, plumbing fixtures.
  • Lighting layers: task, ambient, and statement lighting that works day to night.

Full kitchen remodels in Los Angeles typically run $75,000–$250,000+ depending on size, finishes, and structural scope. Our design fee is separate and scoped to your project—book a call for a clear estimate.

Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions.

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in Los Angeles?

Full kitchen remodels in Los Angeles typically run $75,000–$250,000+ depending on size, structural changes, and finish level. Our design fee is separate, scoped to your project, and covers layout, selections, and trade-ready documentation. We're transparent about fees from the first call—contact us for a clear estimate.

How long does a kitchen remodel take?

Design development with us takes 4–8 weeks. Once construction starts, most kitchen remodels run 8–16 weeks depending on permit requirements, contractor schedules, and scope. Permits in Los Angeles can extend timelines—we plan for this from the beginning so there are no surprises.

Do you design kitchens as part of a full home project?

Yes—kitchens are often the anchor. We can design a kitchen as a standalone scope or as part of a full-service residential project.

Can you work with my contractor or cabinet maker?

Absolutely. We collaborate with your team (or recommend trusted partners) to keep details aligned from drawings through install.

Do you help with appliance selection and integration?

Yes. We plan around appliance specs and coordinate placements so the final look feels seamless and intentional.

How do you choose materials that hold up?

We balance beauty and durability—prioritizing stain resistance, maintenance, and longevity while keeping the aesthetic elevated.

What does "luxury kitchen design" mean in practice?

It means the kitchen is tailored to how you live: clear work zones, storage that reduces clutter, integrated appliances, and a finish palette that feels timeless—not trendy. Luxury is the ease you feel every day.

Do you work outside Los Angeles?

Yes. We're based in Los Angeles and also serve South Florida's Gold Coast, with remote options available depending on scope.
