Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions.

What does a commercial interior design firm do?

A commercial interior design firm handles the full scope of planning and designing non-residential spaces — offices, retail stores, restaurants, hotels, and other commercial environments. This includes space planning, material and finish selection, furniture procurement (FF&E), lighting design, vendor coordination, and install management. JAC Interiors provides all of these services as a full-service commercial interior design company in Los Angeles.

How is commercial interior design different from residential?

Commercial design prioritizes brand alignment, operational efficiency, durability, and compliance with building codes and ADA requirements — in addition to aesthetics. The stakes are higher because the space directly affects employee productivity, customer perception, and long-term maintenance costs. Residential design is more personal; commercial design is more strategic.

How much does commercial interior design cost in Los Angeles?

Commercial design fees vary significantly based on scope, square footage, and level of service. Small office or retail fit-outs may start in the $15,000–$40,000 range for design services, while larger multi-room or multi-location projects scale accordingly. We provide detailed proposals after an initial discovery call so you know exactly what's included.

Can you design within an existing brand guideline?

Yes. We can follow established brand standards — or help refine them into a cohesive spatial experience that extends your brand identity into the physical environment.

Do you work with contractors and vendors?

Absolutely. We coordinate with your general contractor and preferred vendors, or connect you with trusted partners from our network in Los Angeles and South Florida.

Can you design for durability and high traffic?

Yes — materials, finishes, and layouts are selected for longevity and ease of maintenance. We specifically source commercial-grade specifications for high-traffic retail, hospitality, and office environments.

Do you serve areas outside Los Angeles?

Yes. Our commercial interior design services extend throughout Southern California — including Long Beach, Beverly Hills, West Hollywood, Culver City, and surrounding areas — as well as South Florida markets including Miami, Boca Raton, and Fort Lauderdale.

Do you offer remote design support?

Yes. We can run the full design process remotely and coordinate with local contractors for implementation — particularly useful for multi-location commercial clients.
