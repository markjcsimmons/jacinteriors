North Hollywood Interior Design Services

You know that eclectic, storied vibe that just feels like North Hollywood? We do too. At JAC Interiors, we design homes that showcase your personality and reflect the creative undercurrent that runs through every inch of NoHo. Whether you’re nestled near Chandler Estates or off Tujunga, our interior design services reimagine spaces into California-cool reflections of who you are — with unexpected punches of color, layers of texture, and a dose of irreverent charm.

02

North Hollywood as We Know and Love It

North Hollywood isn’t just “up the 170”—it’s an immersive cultural mash-up of creatives, historic architecture, and mid-century palm-lined streets. We’ve watched NoHo transform into a dynamic hub for artists, young professionals, and design-forward homeowners looking to translate their vibe into their living space. Between the art-deco bones of the El Portal Theatre and the sleek new builds cropping up around the NoHo Arts District, we see design opportunity at every corner.

JAC Interiors is a full-service interior design studio with deep roots in Los Angeles, and we’ve worked with clients all through the Valley, including here in North Hollywood. From cozy bungalows in Valley Village to modern remodels off Magnolia Blvd., we tailor every detail to the lifestyle, flow, and creative spirit of the people who live there.

03

The North Hollywood Aesthetic

North Hollywood interiors tend to have more personality than other parts of the Valley — the creative industry presence in NoHo Arts District means clients here have genuine aesthetic opinions and aren’t interested in safe or generic. The mix of vintage character architecture and newer construction creates range: original 1940s bungalows with wood floors and period detail alongside modern remodels with open plans and clean contemporary finishes.

We lean into the creative energy here — layered materials, considered lighting, and furniture that has a point of view. The warm afternoon light in the Valley is one of the neighborhood’s best design assets; we select materials and finishes that translate beautifully across every hour of the day.

04

Designing in North Hollywood

We Balance Urban Grit with Eclectic Cool

From the vintage feel around Lankershim Blvd. to the contemporary lofts near the Red Line station, we love mixing industrial textures with saturated color statements—North Hollywood really calls for it.

We Know How to Work with Historic Spaces

Adding function to charming older homes on Morrison Street, Radford Avenue, or those mid-century flats in Toluca Terrace? We know how to respect the character while enhancing your lifestyle.

Light Matters, and We Get It

The soft, warm light in the Valley hits differently, especially in the late afternoon. We design with that quality in mind—selecting materials and hues that translate beautifully in every hour of the day.

Resourceful and Rooted

We’ve cultivated strong relationships with trusted vendors like CCF Interiors for custom upholstery and Newport Brass & Lee Valley for hardware and faucets.

Local Resources & Collaborators

When sourcing for projects in North Hollywood, we often make design pilgrimages to:

  • Kreiss on Robertson – An iconic showroom that gives us luxe, layered living room vibes, ideal for open-space plans common around Toluca Woods.
  • Modern Lighting on La Brea – sculptural pendants and moody sconces to transform home entries or dining areas.
  • H.D. Buttercup Studio in Culver City is our go-to for modern vintage furniture and one-of-a-kind wood pieces that add warmth to Valley homes.
  • David Homami Meyer on Pico for plumbing hardware—because the right bath fixtures change everything.
