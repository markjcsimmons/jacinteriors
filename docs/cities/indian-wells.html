INDIAN WELLS

Palm Desert is a city in California’s Coachella Valley, near Palm Springs. It’s home to country clubs, golf courses and shops as well as the Coachella Valleys’ famous music festivals. In the center, The Shops on El Paseo feature designer boutiques, plus restaurants and art galleries. The design style of our JAC project in The Madison Club exhibits the long established desert luxury aesthetic married to award winning architecture. Throughout the space JAC hand-picked and curated all materials, lighting and custom furniture. The art was particularly fun to source from both LA and Palm Desert to present a modern and eclectic feel with a gallery-like vision. The house is home to a young professional family and serves as their winter retreat. With indoor outdoor living at it’s best, the space feels double its size and is the perfect place to entertain.
