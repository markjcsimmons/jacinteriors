Wynwood / Edgewater

Wynwood / Edgewater Interior Design Services

At JAC Interiors, our love affair with Miami’s creative heartbeat started in the art-splashed alleys of Wynwood and the shimmering bay views of Edgewater. With our expansion into Florida’s Gold Coast, this vibrant corner of Miami instantly felt like a natural extension of our style-forward, high-touch design approach.

Because Wynwood and Edgewater are brimming with creative energy, textile-rich color palettes, and architecture that dares to dream—from converted warehouse lofts to glass-wrapped penthouses overlooking Biscayne Bay. Whether it’s reimagining an artist’s studio to reflect their eclectic personality or designing a hurricane-proof luxe high-rise, we bring joy, precision, and lifestyle curation to every room.

JAC Interiors—founded by Andrea Putman and Timothy Mersman—offers full-service interior design and design-build services with a West Coast sensibility adapted for South Florida's tropical modern aesthetic. Our solutions are deeply personal, visually captivating, and crafted to last—especially in this climate.

02

The Wynwood & Edgewater Aesthetic

Wynwood and Edgewater sit at opposite ends of the creative-to-refined spectrum, and that tension is exactly what makes them interesting to design for. Wynwood loft conversions ask for bold palettes, high-contrast materials, and gallery-level lighting — spaces that feel like extensions of the murals outside. Edgewater glass-wrapped condos and penthouses get filtered through Biscayne Bay light, which calls for cooler, more luminous finishes that shift throughout the day.

What both neighborhoods share is a resistance to the expected. We source from Wynwood’s own art ecosystem for original work, and from regional makers who understand tropical durability — moisture-resistant veneers, mold-resistant finishes, and materials that handle Florida humidity without sacrificing beauty.

Designing in Wynwood & Edgewater

Designs That Talk Back to the Neighborhood

In Wynwood, we pull cues from local muralists—expressive palettes, high-contrast textures, and gallery-style lighting. In Edgewater, we lean into finishes that play off Biscayne Bay light, from high-gloss terrazzo to tinted glass accents that shift throughout the day.

Coastal-Ready Without Compromise

We choose materials made for Miami—moisture-resistant veneers, mold-resistant paints, and hurricane-impact-rated windows with elegant framing.

Local + Global Vision

We shop Wynwood Walls and Art Basel pop-ups, and we’ll also source one-of-a-kind European finishes when your home calls for it. One favorite local partner is Díaz Display on NE 2nd Avenue.

We Speak “Modern Tropical”

Breezy palettes, sculptural furniture, and biophilic elements that connect with the outdoors—so your space feels relaxed, connected, and a little bit edgy.

Zero Cookie-Cutter. All You.

Your lifestyle is the blueprint. We adapt to your style while elevating flow, storytelling, and functionality.
