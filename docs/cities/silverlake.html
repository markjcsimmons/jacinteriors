Interior Designer Silverlake

Interior Design in Silverlake, Los Angeles

Silverlake is LA's most architecturally restless neighborhood. The hillsides around the reservoir are studded with Neutra houses, Schindler experiments, Craftsman bungalows, Spanish Colonials, and speculative mid-century designs that never quite got labeled — all stacked up on narrow lots with steep driveways and views that stretch to downtown or the hills depending on which way you face.

Designing in Silverlake means working with this variety rather than against it. JAC Interiors approaches each home on its own terms — understanding the specific architectural moment it came from, what the original designer was trying to achieve, and how a contemporary client can live beautifully inside that vision without destroying it.

The Silverlake Design Sensibility

Silverlake owners are among the most design-literate in the city. They've lived with the architecture, understand its references, and have strong opinions about what belongs — and what doesn't. A heavy-handed renovation that replaces original Neutra details with generic contemporary finishes is exactly what they don't want, and it's not what we do.

Our work here tends to be layered and personal — original floors refinished and left exposed, vintage furniture pieces integrated with new custom work, kitchens updated with materials that feel honest to the period while functioning at a modern standard. The goal is a home that feels inhabited and considered, not staged.

04

Why JAC for Your Silverlake Project

We Respect What's Already There

In a neighborhood with this much architectural heritage, the worst thing a designer can do is treat the existing house as a blank canvas. We start by understanding what the home is — its original intent, its best features, its structural quirks — and design from there. Preservation and modernization aren't opposites; in the right hands, they reinforce each other.

Hillside Logistics, Handled

Steep driveways, narrow access, and limited staging areas are the norm in Silverlake. We plan around these constraints from the start — scheduling deliveries thoughtfully, sourcing pieces that can navigate the access, and working with contractors who know the neighborhood's physical realities.

Views as Design Elements

Whether it's the reservoir, downtown, or the hills, Silverlake homes often have views that drive the living arrangement. We design around them — furniture placement, window treatment selection, and lighting all calibrated to keep the view in frame without making the interior feel secondary to it.
