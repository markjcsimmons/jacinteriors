What Do Palm Desert Interior Designers Do?

The interior design creates functional, attractive, and safe spaces while meeting each client's specific requirements and preferences. Using stylish decor, vibrant color palettes, and smart furniture placement, our Palm Desert interior designers can create inviting and efficient spaces - from offices where people love to work to beautiful yet functional living rooms or state-of-the-art kitchens.

At JAC Interiors, we understand that elements such as flooring, lighting, artwork, or window treatments must work together to create a one-of-a-kind look that will satisfy a client's needs. When you come to us, we'll consider the intended function of your space to curate a unique and functional environment that perfectly fits your needs.

Our team views interior design as the art of finding the right colors, the correct lighting, or the right place for the right objects. At JAC Interiors, we will help bring forth your wishes and reflect your identity within your home.

03

The Interior Design Process

At JAC, we start every interior design project by listening to what you hope to achieve through your home remodeling or design. We will then strategize your goals and discuss timelines. Our interior designers will explore your style preferences, lifestyle, and inspirations during this process.

Seeing your house in person will allow us to assess its scale and layout and take measurements for drapery, furnishings, appliances, and bespoke details. During an in-person meeting, our team will present you with a room-by-room visualization of your home, using samples, finishes, and fabric swatches, so that we can create a tactile representation of your new interior.

Once you have selected your furnishings and appliances, we'll handle and manage your orders and have them installed in your home. Contact us today to request an interior design consultation.
