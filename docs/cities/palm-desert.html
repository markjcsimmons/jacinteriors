Palm Desert

Interior Design in Palm Desert

Palm Desert sits at the center of the Coachella Valley and has long been its primary design market. The city draws seasonal residents from Los Angeles and across the country — people for whom a Palm Desert home is often a significant property with expectations to match. The architecture ranges from original mid-century modern homes to contemporary desert compounds, golf course properties at communities like Bighorn and The Reserve, and newer high-spec custom builds throughout the valley.

JAC Interiors provides full-service interior design for Palm Desert homes and seasonal residences. We work with both seasonal residents furnishing a winter retreat and full-time residents undertaking major renovations — and we bring the same rigorous process to both.

02

The Desert Aesthetic

The dominant aesthetic in Palm Desert leans toward warm desert-modern: earthy neutrals, natural travertine and limestone, warm-toned woods, linen and performance fabrics that hold up to the climate without sacrificing luxury. The Coachella Valley sun is intense and directional — managing it through material selection, window treatment, and surface finish is a design discipline in itself. Indoor-outdoor connection isn’t optional here; it’s central to how people actually use these homes.

The valley’s mid-century heritage — the architects and clients who shaped the desert cities in the 1950s and 60s — remains a live influence. Our Palm Desert projects range from updated mid-century interiors that honor the architectural heritage to fully contemporary spaces built around the landscape. What they share is the same approach to quality, proportion, and livability.

Interior Design Services in Palm Desert

Palm Desert is one of Southern California’s most distinctive design markets — and one of its most demanding. Our work here typically includes space planning for open-concept desert floor plans, material and finish selection suited to high UV exposure and extreme temperature ranges, furniture procurement and management, custom window treatments, and complete move-in styling. We also offer home staging and interior design for clients preparing their property for the rental market or for sale.
