Universal City Interior Design Services

And for us at JAC Interiors, that means endless creative opportunities.

Whether you're revamping a contemporary hillside home with dramatic views of the Cahuenga Pass or curating a chic live/work space near the Universal backlot, our approach is always tailored, always elevated, and always fun. Let’s design something unforgettable together (yes, even if you’re just around the corner from Hogwarts™).

02

The Universal City Aesthetic

Universal City sits in a compressed geography — the Hollywood Hills to the south, Studio City across the 101, the Cahuenga Pass threading through. Most of the residential stock is hillside: split-levels, stacked footprints, homes with dramatic views and narrow lots that reward vertical thinking over horizontal sprawl.

The light up here is Southern California at its best — clear, warm, angled through canyon walls. We work with palettes and materials that lean into that rather than compete: soft plasters, natural stone, custom built-ins that maximize unusual floor plans without making them feel cramped. The entertainment industry concentration here also shapes the clientele — these are homes that need to function as creative retreats as much as family residences.

03

Designing in Universal City

Designed for Show-Stopping Moments

Whether you’re hosting an industry dinner or streaming an indie film screener, Universal City homes are meant to dazzle. We create spaces that make an entrance—think bold lighting from Arteriors Melrose or statement marble fireplaces sourced from David Homami Meyer’s Pico showroom.

Smart Solutions for Compact Luxury

In neighborhoods like the Hollywood Dell or hillside pockets near Barham Blvd, where square footage is often wonky or tight, we specialize in maximizing form and function. Custom built-ins, hidden storage, and multi-purpose layouts turn challenges into design triumphs.

Sunlight and Shadow Play

Homes here benefit from that coveted Southern California light—something we love to highlight with smart fabric selections, layered window treatments, and reflective finishes. From modern Spanish to mid-century ranch updates, our palette choices lean into warmth, softness, and dimension.

Entertainment Industry Ease

Many of our Universal City clients juggle high-pressure production roles and off-hours creativity. Our process is streamlined and enjoyable—from our digital mood boards to our trusted contractor network—so you can stay focused on your next big release while we handle the design drama.
