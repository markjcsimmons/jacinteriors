Hollywood Interior Design Services

Elevated, Effortless Living in the Heart of Hollywood, Broward

At JAC Interiors, we bring our signature blend of West Coast vibe, high-function design, and luxury lifestyle to the tropical vibrance of Hollywood, Broward. We know the feeling: you want interiors that feel intentional and elevated, not overly styled or impersonal. Spaces that handle Florida weather but still turn heads. That's where we come in.

This isn’t your cookie-cutter decorating service. JAC is a full-service interior design firm with a stylishly relaxed (but seriously strategic) approach shaped by more than a decade of experience across Los Angeles, Colorado, and now the Gold Coast of Florida. We’re here to help you reimagine life at home by designing layered interiors that reflect your taste and work seamlessly with South Florida’s lifestyle—from sun-soaked family rooms to hurricane-ready finishes that give you peace of mind during storm season.

Whether your place is perched along North Ocean Drive, nestled in Emerald Hills, or a modern build just west of Young Circle, our team understands the nuances of Hollywood’s indoor-outdoor rhythm and how to design for it intelligently—with style, not stress.

Why JAC Interiors is Hollywood’s Insider Design Firm

1. Deep Design Fluency Meets Local Context

With decades of design experience from L.A. to Delray Beach, we understand how South Florida’s humidity, coastal breezes, and sunlight demand different materials and configurations. Our team selects finishes that are as resilient as they are stylish.

2. Intentionally Curated, Never Cookie-Cutter

Hollywood homes vary wildly—from mid-century stunners off Johnson Street to newer construction near West Lake Park. That’s why every JAC home is distinct. We tailor every design plan to complement your home’s bones, your personal aesthetic, and your lifestyle goals.

3. From Drawings to Finishes—We Handle It All

We don’t just style spaces, we design them from the ground up. Whether it’s a major bathroom remodel near Hollywood Beach or a full-scale built-in bookshelf in Lakes of Emerald Hills, we bring your ideas to life while managing every detail.

4. Vendor Access Without the Stress

Our strong network of exceptional vendors means your project benefits from premium materials, custom furniture, and statement lighting that’s not found in retail showrooms. Bonus: we handle all the scheduling, ordering, and logistics.

5. Collaborative, Down-to-Earth Vibes

You’ll find us somewhere between creative visionary and logistical powerhouse. We’re here to make the design process enjoyable. Our clients feel relaxed and empowered by our process—we treat every project like it’s our own home.

Our Go-To Local Vendors & Collaborators in Hollywood

We’re often sourcing one-of-a-kind marble slabs and exotic woods through MOD Construction Supply in Fort Lauderdale, or working hand-in-glove with the expert cabinetry team at Luxe Kitchen & Bath in Dania Beach. For contemporary lighting installations, Arteriors and Lumens’ South Florida divisions deliver sophistication and function in equal parts.

Our team is also known to make a pit stop at Deco Domicile off Federal Highway—because their curated collection of mid-century and Brazilian modern furniture is basically a playground for design lovers.

And for outdoor spaces, we source luxury, coastal-rated materials perfect for balconies, pool decks, and transitional patios from Tropical Hardwoods in Miami.

Designing Across Hollywood’s Diverse Neighborhoods

Every pocket of Hollywood in Broward tells a different design story:

  • Historic vibes and character-rich homes in Hollywood Lakes offer incredible opportunities for modern-meets-vintage renovations.
  • Contemporary rebuilds in Emerald Hills call for sleek finishes and smarter space planning—especially for growing families.
  • Hollywood Beach condos inspire a fresh, modern coastal palette—think driftwood tones, breezy textures, and hurricane-tough materials.
  • Pinehurst or Hillcrest? We know how to work with HOA requirements while still creating high-end, ultra-custom results.

From East Hollywood to Boulevard Heights, we bring a flexible, insightful approach to every project, working within neighborhood constraints without sacrificing on style.
