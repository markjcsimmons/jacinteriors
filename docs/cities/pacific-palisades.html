Rebuild LA, Re‑Imagine Home

Professional interior design & finish-spec services for wildfire-recovery builds.

Los Angeles is still healing from recent wildfire seasons. Whether you’re a home-owner starting over, a developer filling critical housing gaps, or an architect/GC managing multiple rebuilds, JAC Interiors brings clarity and design excellence to every square foot. Our mission: help you rebuild faster, within budget, and more beautifully—without surprises.

  • Milestone walk‑throughs to keep schedules on track

Pricing

Simple transparent pricing.

Our pricing is a simple, per-square-foot model that keeps every project predictable from day one. You choose the tier that matches your goals—Essential for rapid, budget-savvy rebuilds, Premium for forever homes and flagship specs, or Signature for fully bespoke, showcase spaces. Each tier’s price covers our full design fee plus the specification and procurement of all hard-scape finishes; contractor labor and furnishings are separate.
