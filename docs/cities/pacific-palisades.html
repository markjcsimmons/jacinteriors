Simple Transparent Pricing

Our pricing is a simple, per-square-foot model that keeps every project predictable from day one. You choose the tier that matches your goals—Essential for rapid, budget-savvy rebuilds, Premium for forever homes and flagship specs, or Signature for fully bespoke, showcase spaces. Each tier’s price covers our full design fee plus the specification and procurement of all hard-scape finishes; contractor labor and furnishings are separate.

Tier Best For Material Profile Investment*
ESSENTIAL Rapid rebuilds Quality factory-fabricated finishes (porcelain, engineered stone) from $100 / sq ft
PREMIUM Forever homes & flagship spec houses Mix of custom cabinetry & natural stone/wood ≈ $200 / sq ft
SIGNATURE Showcase luxury properties Fully bespoke, artisan-crafted interiors from $300 / sq ft
