Las Olas / Victoria Park Interior Design Services

At JAC Interiors, we love designing spaces that evoke emotion, tell your story, and reflect the vibrant lifestyle of Las Olas and Victoria Park. With our expansion into Florida’s Gold Coast, we’re bringing our brand of laid-back luxury to these sun-drenched streets.

We know these neighborhoods intimately. Whether it’s a breezy canal-front villa along Mola Avenue or a mid-century charmer nestled off NE 9th in Victoria Park, JAC Interiors helps homeowners embrace the rhythm of Fort Lauderdale’s east side—with interiors that are both elevated and livable.

With Florida humidity and year-round sunshine informing every design decision, our interiors are built beautifully and built smart—with materials that stand up to salt air, sandy feet, and continuous entertaining.

02

The Las Olas / Victoria Park Aesthetic

The dominant aesthetic in these neighborhoods runs from Mediterranean Revival — the ornate archways, barrel-tile roofs, and courtyard-facing rooms of the Las Olas Isles and Nurmi Isles estates — to the clean mid-century ranch and contemporary infill that define much of Victoria Park. Each type has its own logic and its own design opportunities, but the common thread is South Florida light: bright, directional, and central to every interior decision.

Coastal conditions shape material choices throughout — fabrics that resist humidity, metals that hold up to salt air, finishes that age gracefully in the sun. Our work here combines California-trained design sensibility with a genuine understanding of how Broward’s climate and lifestyle inform livable luxury.

03

Designing in Las Olas / Victoria Park

Architectural Insiders

The homes off Las Olas Boulevard and in Victoria Park offer unique opportunities—from Mediterranean-style estates with ornate archways to the clean lines of modern canal homes. Our team honors each property’s character while updating it for contemporary living.

Durable, Luxury Living

Salt air, strong sunlight, and the occasional tropical storm are just part of the territory here. We source hurricane-smart materials and finishes that maintain their integrity and beauty through it all.

Local, But Luxe

We partner with trusted South Florida vendors — from high-gloss lacquer cabinetry in Pompano to custom millwork out of Fort Lauderdale’s Flagler Village. Our on-the-ground presence means smoother timelines and less stress.

Daylight-Driven Design

The natural light in this part of Fort Lauderdale is irresistible. From soft linen sheers along the Intracoastal to kitchen layouts that capture golden hour in Colee Hammock, we create rooms that respond to the sunlight as much as your lifestyle.

Our Process

Every Las Olas and Victoria Park project starts with a site visit and a conversation about the space: what’s working, what you’ve been wanting to change, and how you actually live there day-to-day. We develop a design concept — space planning, material and finish selections, furniture direction — and present it as a cohesive proposal before anything moves forward. From there, we manage procurement, contractor coordination, and installation through to final styling.
