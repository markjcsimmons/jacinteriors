Las Olas / Victoria Park Interior Design Services

Curated Coastal Interiors That Celebrate South Florida Living

At JAC Interiors, we love designing spaces that evoke emotion, tell your story, and reflect the vibrant lifestyle of Las Olas and Victoria Park. With our recent expansion into Florida’s Gold Coast, we’re bringing our signature brand of laid-back luxury to these sun-drenched streets.

We know these neighborhoods intimately. Whether it’s a breezy canal-front villa along Mola Avenue or a mid-century charmer nestled off NE 9th in Victoria Park, JAC Interiors helps homeowners embrace the rhythm of Fort Lauderdale’s east side—with interiors that are both elevated and livable.

With the Florida humidity and year-round sunshine informing every design decision, our interiors are built beautifully—but also built smart—with materials that stand up to salt air, sandy feet, and continuous entertaining. Think hurricane-smart finishes, statement lighting that plays with coastal daylight, and interiors that flow seamlessly to outdoor spaces.

We approach every project the way we’d design our own homes—thoughtfully, collaboratively, and with just the right amount of boldness.

Why Choose JAC Interiors for Your Las Olas / Victoria Park Home?

1. We’re Architectural Insiders

The homes off Las Olas Boulevard and in Victoria Park offer unique opportunities—from Mediterranean-style estates with ornate archways to the clean lines of modern canal homes. Our team uses design-build expertise to honor each property’s character while updating it to meet contemporary living needs.

2. Durable, Luxury Living

Salt air, strong sunlight, and the occasional tropical storm are just part of the territory here. As your Las Olas interior designer, we source hurricane-smart materials and finishes that maintain their integrity and beauty—because design should withstand the weather as much as the seasons of life.

3. Local, But Luxe

We partner with trusted South Florida vendors for premium materials—from high-gloss lacquer cabinetry sourced locally in Pompano to custom millwork out of Fort Lauderdale’s Flagler Village. Our on-the-ground presence means smoother timelines and less stress.

4. Design That Feels Like You—Only Better

Victoria Park’s diverse architecture—from charming 1930s cottages to ultramodern infill townhomes—means no two homes are alike. That’s why we don’t have a “one-style-fits-all” mentality. We listen, get to know your lifestyle, and infuse function with soul.

5. Daylight-Driven Design

The natural light in this part of Fort Lauderdale is irresistible. From filtering soft linen sheers along the Intracoastal to designing kitchen layouts that capture golden hour in Colee Hammock, we create rooms that respond to the sunlight as much as your lifestyle.
