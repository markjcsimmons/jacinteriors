Bel Air

Bel Air Interior Design Services

As long-time Angelenos and design aficionados, JAC Interiors knows that Bel Air is more than a zip code, it’s a state of mind. Tucked into the Santa Monica Mountains with sweeping canyon views and old-world glamour, Bel Air demands a refined sensibility that honors both legacy and innovation. Whether we’re inside a gated Spanish Revival on Bellagio Road or a sleek modern estate along St. Pierre, JAC Interiors brings warmth, charisma, and effortless luxury to every home.

From managing intense Southern California sunlight to designing interiors that soften the glass-and-steel contemporary lines so iconic to the area, our full-service design team understands what it takes to make a Bel Air home feel grounded, yet elevated. We approach every project with empathy, taste, and an eye for livable artistry.

02

The Bel Air Aesthetic

Bel Air is one of LA’s oldest luxury addresses, and the homes reflect that — gated estates, sweeping canyon views, architectural vocabulary ranging from Spanish Revival to glass-and-steel contemporary. What most of them share is scale: high ceilings, generous proportions, rooms that demand furniture and finishes that match their ambition.

Managing Southern California light is central to every Bel Air project. The sun exposure here is intense, and the glass walls on contemporary builds can make interiors feel harsh without deliberate softening — the right window treatments, material palette, and spatial layout matter more here than almost anywhere else we work. We bring that calibration to every project, balancing the drama of the architecture with the warmth that makes a home feel genuinely livable.

Local Resources & Collaborators

We’re constantly pulled into inspiration at showrooms like Woven on Beverly, where the custom rugs ground our Bel Air designs with texture and artistry. Our custom upholstery partners in Culver City deliver bespoke sofas that reshape traditional Bel Air living rooms into editorial spaces with heart. For kitchens and bath projects, we lean on the offerings at Snyder Diamond in Santa Monica, it’s our top pick when we need luxury fittings that don’t feel overdone.

The Arteriors showroom in the La Cienega Design District is another favorite. We’ve turned to them for statement lighting in homes off Chalon Road, where an airy open-plan needs a bold sculptural fixture to create visual punctuation.
