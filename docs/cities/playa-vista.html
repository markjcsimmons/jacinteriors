Playa Vista Interior Design Services

Welcome to Playa Vista, home of tech meets texture, where innovation pulses through Runway’s cafes and design-forward living is just a way of life. At JAC Interiors, we’re not just interior designers—we’re your Playa Vista neighbors, perched right alongside you in this vibrant, future-thinking enclave of the Westside. Whether you’re in a modern New Urbanism condo along Bluff Creek Drive or an elegant home in The Collection, our Playa Vista interior design services blend laid-back luxury with personalized artistry.

With the neighborhood’s signature warm coastal light, proximity to the ocean’s breezes, and the sleek, often minimalistic architectural styles, interior design in Playa Vista calls for a nuanced approach. Our goal? To create bold, comfortable, personality-packed spaces that reflect you, while honoring the upscale, modern vibe of local living.

02

The Playa Vista Aesthetic

Playa Vista is a different kind of Westside neighborhood — planned, modern, and built around a lifestyle that leans into tech, community, and clean design. The architecture is predominantly New Urbanism: open plans, oversized windows, contemporary finishes. Interiors here work best when they lean into that language rather than fight it.

We design for residents who are visual, discerning, and engaged in the process. That means layering warmth into spaces that could otherwise feel too clean — natural textures, curated art, custom millwork that gives a condo the feel of a house. The light here is coastal and generous; the palette tends modern, with moments of material richness that make each space feel personal rather than generic.

Designing in Playa Vista

Understanding of Urban + Tech-Influenced Architecture

Tailored Designs for Westside Living

Close to the beach yet nestled in upwardly mobile neighborhoods, your home has to breathe. We layer airy material palettes, clean lines, and natural textures to complement Play Vista’s climate and light.

Smart Design for Smart Homes

With many Playa Vista clients hailing from nearby tech and media giants (hey, Google and YouTube!), we integrate design-forward, tech-savvy features that don’t skimp on style.

Our Insider Resources, At Your Doorstep

We source furniture, art, and accessories from cult-favorite LA vendors—think curated finds from Big Daddy’s Antiques on Jefferson Blvd, low-profile seating and sculptural lighting from Organic Modernism in Culver City.

We Build Relationships, Not Just Rooms

Our Playa Vista clients tend to be well-informed, discerning, and engaged—and that’s our perfect match. We co-create spaces that feel like an extension of you, elevated where it counts, relaxed where it matters.

Local Resources & Collaborators We Love

We’re regulars at A + R (Arts + Revolution) in Venice for that wow-factor piece that makes a room unforgettable. Their micro-edited showroom is perfect for Playa Vista homeowners seeking edgy, design-forward accessories or lighting.

For clients remodeling kitchens or baths, we partner with the team at Ferguson on Washington Blvd in Marina del Rey, they’re practically family, helping us source statement faucets, artisan tiles, and under-the-radar Italian hardware brands that set your Playa Vista space apart from the rest.

And when it comes to textiles, Kat & Roger Ceramics (available at Midland Shop in Culver City) is our go-to for style-forward, locally made finishing touches.
