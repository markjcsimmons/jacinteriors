Interior Design in Los Feliz, Los Angeles

Los Feliz occupies a singular position in the LA residential landscape. Wedged between Griffith Park to the north, Silverlake to the south, and Hollywood to the west, it has been a destination for architects, artists, and industry professionals since the 1920s. The housing stock reflects that history: Spanish Colonial Revivals, Greek Revivals, Craftsman bungalows, and the occasional Lloyd Wright–era home — all on tree-lined streets that feel genuinely unlike any other part of the city.

JAC Interiors designs for Los Feliz owners who understand their homes' character and want to work with it, not over it. Our approach here is sympathetic to the original architecture — we don't strip away what makes these houses special in pursuit of a generic "modern" look. We help them evolve.

Working with Pre-War Architecture

Los Feliz homes present a specific set of opportunities and constraints. Original plasterwork, hardwood floors, arched doorways, and built-in cabinetry are assets — the kind of character that new construction costs a fortune to replicate. But older homes also come with smaller rooms by today's standards, compartmentalized floor plans, and electrical and mechanical systems that need updating.

We navigate this terrain carefully. Kitchens and bathrooms get updated for modern function while keeping the proportions and material palette honest to the era. New lighting is layered in without destroying original plaster ceilings. Paint colors and fabric selections are calibrated to the particular quality of light that filters through the mature tree canopy on most Los Feliz streets — softer and more filtered than what you find in open Westside neighborhoods.

Vintage + New — Without the Clash
