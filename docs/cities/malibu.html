Malibu

Malibu Interior Design

Malibu interior design is about one thing: the site. Whether a home sits above PCH on a Malibu Road promontory, backs to a creek in the Carbon Mesa hills, or opens directly onto the sand at Malibu Colony, the relationship between interior and exterior — the light, the view, the ocean proximity — defines every design decision. JAC Interiors brings full-service residential design to Malibu with deep familiarity with what the California coastal environment demands.

We’ve worked on a range of Malibu project types: contemporary builds with glass walls and steel structure, post-fire rebuilds through our Rebuild LA program, and traditional beach homes updated for current living without losing the warmth that made them work. The common thread is close attention to the site and the light — which are unlike anywhere else in LA.

02

The Malibu Aesthetic

Malibu has a design logic distinct from the rest of coastal LA. Homes along PCH and on the bluffs above it face the ocean directly — full sun exposure, wind, salt air, and the constant visual competition of the Pacific. Finishes have to be beautiful and durable. Materials that look weathered-in rather than weathered-out. Upholstery rated for UV. Hardware that won't corrode in the marine layer.

The palette runs natural: warm whites, wood tones, stone, woven textiles that echo the landscape without being literal about it. The best Malibu interiors feel like they grew from the site — as if removing the furniture would reveal the same material logic in the landscape beyond the glass. We work toward that coherence, rather than imposing a design concept onto a location that already has a strong character of its own.

03

Home Types We Design For

Malibu's residential geography is more varied than it appears from PCH. The Colony and Carbon Beach are dense beachfront — tight lots, shared walls on one side, the ocean on the other. Malibu Road and the Paradise Cove bluffs offer more separation and elevation, with sites that frame the water rather than sitting in it. Further inland, Malibu Park and the roads above Kanan Dume bring a wilder character, chaparral-adjacent and sometimes with almost no ocean visibility.

These contexts call for different approaches. A Colony beachfront home requires durability and easy maintenance as much as beauty; a bluff-top build can prioritize the view in ways a strand property can't; a mountain-area home above town might have almost nothing to do with the ocean aesthetically, even though it's technically in Malibu. We design for all of these, and we bring that site-specific thinking to every project we take on here.

Our Process

Every Malibu project starts with the site — how it faces, what it sees, what the coastal climate does to materials throughout the year. From there we move through space planning, finish selection, procurement, and installation, managing every trade and vendor relationship throughout.
