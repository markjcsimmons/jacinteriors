Welcome to JAC Interiors in Coral Springs – Where Your Lifestyle Meets Luxury

There’s something timelessly charming about Coral Springs. Whether it’s the mature oak-lined streets of Whispering Woods or the tranquil lakefront lots in Heron Bay, this city has always held a unique spot in South Florida’s design landscape. At JAC Interiors, we’re thrilled to bring our luxury interior design services to Coral Springs—where our sunny, high-finish aesthetic pairs perfectly with the tropical lushness and architectural diversity of this Broward gem.

We’ve built our reputation (from Los Angeles to Frisco and now Florida’s Gold Coast) by crafting elevated spaces that draw from the local vernacular—spaces that answer to the unique lifestyle and climate challenges of the communities we serve. In Coral Springs, that means interiors designed with hurricane-ready durability, natural light maximization, and a seamless indoor-outdoor flow that supports the Florida way of living, every season of the year.

Why Choose JAC Interiors in Coral Springs?

1. South Florida-Smart Design

Coral Springs’ climate calls for more than just pretty spaces—we design with purpose. Our projects here incorporate materials and finishes that resist warping from humidity, fade resistance for abundant year-round sun, and finishes that allow homes to weather the storm season in both style and safety.

2. Insider Access to the Best Local Artisans & Vendors

We’ve cultivated an enviable insider network across Broward County. For kitchen remodels, we often visit Miele at the Design District and Ferguson’s Sunrise location for appliances and plumbing fixtures. When we need that impossible-to-find accessory or lighting piece for a Coral Springs client? We call on curated showrooms in nearby Boca Raton like MOD Shop or Addison House.

3. Interior Design That Respects Your Architecture

We respect the variety here. From the Mid-Century Florida homes tucked in Forest Hills to the modern Mediterranean estates of Eagle Trace, we design interiors that complement—not compete—with the bones of the home. It’s all about creating harmony between structure and style.

4. Stress-Free, Full-Service Process

Our “Crafty Hustle” approach means you're never bogged down by the logistics. JAC Interiors manages your entire design journey—from rendering to reveal—with obsessive attention to detail and deep local coordination.

5. Local Presence, National Experience

Though we hail from design meccas like Los Angeles and the Rockies, Florida’s Gold Coast is more than just our expansion—it’s personal. With boots on the ground and sunlight in our approach, we design Coral Springs homes with beach-tuned style and seamless project flow.

Local Resources & Collaborators We Love

One of our favorite things about designing in Coral Springs is how generous the local design community is. When we’re looking for standout slabs for a luxury kitchen install, we consult with our trusted reps at Marble of the World in Pompano Beach. For architectural fixtures and decorative details, Allied Plumbing in Fort Lauderdale is our go-to collaborator.

We love sourcing textiles from Designers Resource Center to incorporate breezy, durable fabrics that stand up to Florida conditions. And for original art and accessories? We could spend entire afternoons at ArtServe in Fort Lauderdale or scouting local galleries along Las Olas.
