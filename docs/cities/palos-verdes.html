Interior Design in Palos Verdes

The Palos Verdes Peninsula rises dramatically from the South Bay, and from nearly any point on the hill you can see the Pacific, Catalina Island, and on clear days, far down the coast. It's one of the few places in Los Angeles where the landscape genuinely commands the design of the homes that sit in it—views this sweeping don't ask to be ignored or merely acknowledged. They shape the architecture and, by extension, everything we do inside.

At JAC Interiors, we bring full-service interior design to the peninsula across all its distinct communities—Rancho Palos Verdes, Palos Verdes Estates, Rolling Hills Estates, and Rolling Hills—each with its own character and home type.

02

Designing Around the View

Ocean-view homes in Palos Verdes present a specific design challenge: the view is the dominant feature, and everything else has to serve it without competing. We work with lower furniture profiles that preserve sight lines, glass and reflective surfaces that extend the sense of space, and color palettes that complement the Pacific light rather than fighting it. Marine-grade materials that hold up to salt air are a practical necessity, and we source them as part of every project.

For homes on the quieter interior streets—the equestrian properties in Rolling Hills, the mature canyon lots—the design shifts toward warmth and enclosure: natural stone, rich wood tones, and interiors that feel settled and grounded.

03

Home Types on the Peninsula

Palos Verdes has a strong stock of mid-century ranch homes—generous single-story floor plans with good bones that respond well to thoughtful updating. Open-plan living areas, large windows, and connection to the garden or pool are already part of the architecture; our work is to update finishes, improve the kitchen and bathrooms, and furnish in a way that feels contemporary without erasing the mid-century character.

Newer custom builds and estate-scale renovations are also a significant part of our work here—projects where the scope covers every room and requires the kind of coordination across trades and timelines that only a full-service studio handles well.

04

Our Process

Every Palos Verdes project begins with a site visit—we want to understand the light, the views, and how you live in the space before we develop a single recommendation. From there, we build a full plan covering layout, finish selections, furnishing, and complete installation coordination.
