Service overview

A layout that works before anything else goes in.

Space planning resolves the foundational questions — where things go, how rooms connect, and how people move through the space — so every subsequent decision builds on a solid foundation.

  1. Discovery — We review your measurements, existing furniture, and how each room needs to function.
  2. Planning — We develop layout options, testing furniture arrangements, clearances, and traffic paths.
  3. Delivery — You receive a clear floor plan with annotated recommendations you can act on immediately.

What you get

  • Scaled floor plans: accurate layouts with furniture sized and positioned correctly.
  • Traffic flow analysis: clear paths that prevent bottlenecks and improve daily ease.
  • Furniture placement: arrangements that maximise function and create natural conversation areas.
  • Storage solutions: built-in and freestanding options that reduce visual clutter.
Best for

Any room that feels off — whether it's a new space you're moving into or an existing room that has never quite worked.

Works well as: a standalone engagement or the first step in a broader design project.

Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions.

Do I need to know what furniture I want before starting?

No — space planning works best before furniture decisions are made. We help you understand what fits and how to arrange it optimally.

Can you work with my existing furniture?

Yes. We can plan around pieces you're keeping and identify gaps or replacements as needed.

Is space planning available as a standalone service?

Yes. It can be a one-time engagement or the starting point for a broader design project.

Can this be done remotely?

Yes — with accurate measurements and photos, space planning works well as a remote service.
