Service overview

Design the heart of the home.

We start with function—how you move through the space, what you store, and how you entertain—then layer in cabinetry, finishes, and lighting that elevate the entire home.

  1. Flow & zoning — Islands, walkways, and work triangles planned for the way you cook and gather.
  2. Cabinetry & storage — Thoughtful storage, clean elevations, and details that feel custom.
  3. Finishes & lighting — Durable, timeless materials and lighting that works for task and atmosphere.

What you get

  • Layout + space planning: plans that optimize flow, sightlines, and storage.
  • Cabinetry direction: elevations, hardware, and functional details.
  • Finish selections: countertops, backsplash, flooring, paint, and fixtures.
  • Lighting plan: layered lighting for task, ambient, and statement moments.

Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions.

Do you design kitchens as part of a full home project?

Yes—kitchens are often the anchor. We can design a kitchen as a standalone scope or as part of a full-service residential project.

Can you work with my contractor or cabinet maker?

Absolutely. We collaborate with your team (or recommend trusted partners) to keep details aligned from drawings through install.

Do you help with appliance selection and integration?

Yes. We plan around appliance specs and coordinate placements so the final look feels seamless and intentional.

How do you choose materials that hold up?

We balance beauty and durability—prioritizing stain resistance, maintenance, and longevity while keeping the aesthetic elevated.
