Service overview

Expert direction—without a full project commitment.

Consulting gives you clarity fast. We’ll help you resolve key decisions like layout, paint, finishes, furniture direction, and “what to do next.”

  1. Pre-work — You share photos, measurements, inspiration, and goals.
  2. Consultation — We review options, troubleshoot, and align on a clear direction.
  3. Next steps — You leave with an actionable plan and prioritized recommendations.

What you get

  • Clear direction: design decisions you can move forward with confidently.
  • Recommendations: curated suggestions for paint, finishes, and furniture.
  • Layout feedback: practical improvements to flow and function.
  • Action plan: prioritized next steps to keep momentum.
Best for

Quick clarity on a room or project—especially if you’re stuck between options.

Great for: paint & finishes, furniture layouts, styling direction, vendor decisions.

Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions.

What should I prepare before our consultation?

Photos, rough measurements, inspiration, and a list of decisions you’re stuck on. We’ll guide the rest.

Can you advise on paint colors and finishes?

Yes—paint, materials, and finish cohesion are common consulting topics.

Is consulting available remotely?

Yes. Remote sessions work well with photos, measurements, and virtual walkthroughs.

Can consulting lead into full-service design?

Absolutely. Many clients start with consulting, then expand to a full-service scope when ready.
